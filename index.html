Topic Areas

Where I work and speak

My work sits at the intersection of enterprise AI, intelligent automation, low-code development, and agentic systems.

I focus on how organizations can move from experimentation and individual copilots toward scalable AI and automation capabilities that work across applications, data, processes, and teams. From low-code solutions to autonomous agents and enterprise-wide orchestration, I connect strategic direction with the practical realities of architecture, implementation, governance, security, operations, and adoption.

AI Foundry & Agentic AI

Designing and building autonomous and multi-agent systems with Microsoft AI Foundry, Azure AI, and the broader Microsoft agent ecosystem.

Topics include agent architecture, reasoning and orchestration patterns, grounding and enterprise data integration, model selection, tools and actions, multi-agent collaboration, observability, evaluation, security, and responsible AI. I’m particularly interested in what happens after the prototype: how organizations operate, govern, monitor, scale, and financially manage agentic AI in production.

Copilot Studio

Building enterprise agents and conversational AI experiences with Microsoft Copilot Studio.

This includes custom agents, voice-enabled experiences, autonomous agents, agent flows, test automation, connectors and integrations, multi-agent orchestration, and extending Microsoft 365 Copilot with organization-specific knowledge and actions.

Beyond building the agent itself, I focus on the enterprise lifecycle around it: environments, ALM, governance, security, testing, monitoring, adoption, and establishing repeatable patterns for moving agents from experimentation into production.

Power Platform

Using Power Apps, Power Automate, Power Pages, Dataverse, and the wider Power Platform to turn business processes into scalable digital solutions.

My focus extends beyond individual apps and flows to enterprise automation architecture: combining low-code, APIs, connectors, cloud services, RPA, AI, agents, and human-in-the-loop processes. This also includes governance, ALM, environment strategy, reusable components, Center of Excellence approaches, and designing automation that remains maintainable as adoption grows.

Microsoft 365 Copilot

Helping organizations turn Microsoft 365 Copilot from an individual productivity tool into part of a broader enterprise AI transformation.

Topics include adoption strategies, use-case discovery, Copilot extensibility, agents, enterprise search and knowledge, governance, information readiness, security, and measuring business value. I’m especially interested in how Microsoft 365 Copilot connects employees, organizational knowledge, business processes, and specialized agents to create new ways of working across the enterprise.

Azure AI Services

Integrating Azure AI capabilities into real-world business processes and enterprise applications.

This covers language, speech, vision, document processing, search, content understanding, and other cognitive capabilities, as well as how they can complement generative AI and agentic architectures.

The emphasis is on composable solutions: combining AI services, enterprise data, APIs, automation, agents, and existing applications rather than treating AI as an isolated technology. Architecture, security, scalability, responsible AI, observability, and operational cost are important parts of that conversation.

Dynamics 365

Transforming CRM, customer service, and contact centre operations with AI, automation, Copilot, and agents.

My focus includes Dynamics 365 Customer Service, AI-supported service processes, conversational and voice experiences, case and knowledge automation, intelligent routing, agent assistance, and automation across the customer journey.

A key theme is connecting Dynamics 365 with Copilot Studio, Power Platform, Azure AI, Microsoft 365, and enterprise systems to create end-to-end processes where humans, copilots, automation, and autonomous agents can work together.

The common thread

Across all six areas, the question I’m interested in is increasingly the same:

How do we move from isolated AI demos and automations to a governed, scalable enterprise capability where people, copilots, agents, data, and business processes work together?

That means looking beyond the technology itself and addressing the things that ultimately determine whether enterprise AI succeeds: architecture, governance, security, observability, FinOps, lifecycle management, adoption, and measurable business value.
